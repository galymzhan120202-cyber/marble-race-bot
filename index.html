      ALL YOU NEED TO KNOW

      Pick. Watch. Cheer.

      1. 01
        Choose your favourite.

        Every racer has its own weight and personality. There’s no steering — they find their own way. Quick race launches your saved setup.

      2. 02
        Make it your race.

        Pick an arena and invite 2–8 racers. Play one race, or a three-round Cup with the same crew. The podium earns 5, 3, and 1 points; the highest total wins.

      3. 03
        Watch it unfold.

        Follow the leader or your racer. Pause, change playback speed, or share a link to replay the same race. Speed changes how fast you watch, not the result.

      If time runs out, the furthest-progress racer leads. Cup points go only to racers who cross the finish. Picking a character doesn’t give them an advantage.
